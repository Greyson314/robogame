# Visuals note — the inventor aesthetic

> User's direction note, July 4, 2026 (session 131). This supersedes the
> origami/papercraft direction as the vibe target — the inventor aesthetic
> is its "more fun spiritual twin." Treat this as the steer for future
> art-direction work; the engineering rules in
> [../subsystems/art-direction.md](../subsystems/art-direction.md)
> (MaterialPropertyBlock-only, perf budgets) remain in force. The
> paper-punk weapon family built in session 131 stays in-game as interim
> assets and much of its language (accent restraint, cartoon proportions,
> visible construction) carries forward in wood + linen.

The game will be a daedalus/da vinci-style inventor core. Since the game
is slapstick and silly it doesn't need to hold religiously to any time
period or *particular* style (e.g. maybe we could use tesla coils or
something for batteries), but that will generally be the aesthetic.
Deeply musical vibe, too: I'm thinking a bit like how Don't Starve's
characters speak in instruments; I'd love it if you fire a volley of
mortars and you mostly hear a piano flourish on shot + landing, for
example.

## Features

- Rib-and-membrane flight surfaces
- The aerial screw, rib-and-membrane flapping wings
- Wood + linen aesthetic throughout
- Maybe a stretch-visual: Inkiness, Hwei-from-League style.
  Ideas-coming-to-life aesthetic.
  - NB: Fantasia/Sorcerer's-Apprentice stuff-coming-to-life isn't out of
    the picture
- Map aesthetic: aiming for inkiness and a drawn kind of stylization,
  for now
  - From Claude: "**Ink as the da Vinci layer instead of filigree.** This
    is the answer to 'how do you get renaissance without ornament':
    construction marks. Registration ticks, dashed fold lines,
    measurement annotations, part numbers printed on the card faces — the
    bot looks like it came straight off the drafting table, which closes
    the loop with the blueprint build mode (the sketch became the
    machine, and you can still see the pencil marks). Cheap texture-stage
    work, and it obeys the accent-restraint discipline we set with the
    vermilion. Small easter egg begging to be included: da Vinci
    annotated in mirror writing. **A damage story for free.**
    Skeleton-and-skin gives you two-stage destruction that's readable at
    distance and on-pillar for slapstick: membrane tears first (holes in
    the paper skin, ribs showing through), then spars snap. A shot-up
    ornithopter limping home with tattered wings is both a health bar and
    a joke."
