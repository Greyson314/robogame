## Context

What's the situation that forces a decision? Constraints, prior art,
the failure mode we're trying to avoid. Keep this to a few paragraphs
— the goal is enough context for someone reading this two years from
now to understand why the choice was load-bearing.

## Decision

The choice, stated as a single sentence if possible. What we *will*
do, not "we should consider doing."

## Alternatives considered

The runner-up options and why they lost. This is the part that pays
off the most when someone is later tempted to redo the work — it
should tell them what they'd need to demonstrate to change the
answer.

## Consequences

What this commits us to. Positive and negative, short term and
long term. If this ADR creates new invariants or budgets, name them
here and cross-link to `invariants.md` or the relevant subsystem doc.

## Notes

Optional. Links to the session log that landed it, related ADRs,
related PRs.
